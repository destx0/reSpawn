# Design Context

## Design Context

### Users

Developers using Redux, Redux Toolkit, NgRx or other store-devtools clients. They open the Alt P palette mid-task, usually in a dark office and often while the DevTools panel is docked. They need to find, save, restore or navigate to a checkpoint without losing their place in the app.

### Brand Personality

Precise, calm and instrument-like. Warm rather than clinical; confident but quiet. Not playful, not decorative, not a generic Spotlight clone.

### Aesthetic Direction

Monochrome black and white. Different shades of neutral gray carry the hierarchy; no hue, no brand color. Fine hairlines, full-ring selection, a slightly sunken toolbar and plain paper backdrops in design studies. No labels, keycaps or footer notation inside the palette. The DevTools panel has its own visual language and is not part of this direction.

### Design Principles

1. Treat the palette as an instrument: fast scanning, clear hierarchy, keyboard-first.
2. Stay monochrome. White (dark mode) or black (light mode) marks the primary action, the selected row and the active tool; nothing else competes.
3. Build structure from hairlines, rings, spacing and weight, not from section labels or boxes.
4. Keep the palette legible on any page backdrop; it must hold its own without relying on the page theme.
5. Stay minimal in content but not flat in surface: neutral grays, subtle depth, no noise or glow for its own sake.
